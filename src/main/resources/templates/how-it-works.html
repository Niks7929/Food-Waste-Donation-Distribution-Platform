Step-by-Step Architecture

How the Food Rescue Process Works

Explore how each stakeholder interacts with the system from publication to final distribution.

1. Donor Workflow

  1. Account Verification: Donor registers and admin verifies commercial organization license.
  2. Create Food Listing: Enter food name, category, veg/non-veg status, quantity (meals, kg, packets), preparation time, allergen notes, and pickup deadline.
  3. Review Inbound Requests: Receive instant notification when an NGO requests a batch; review and approve or reject.
  4. Handover to Courier: Handoff packed meals to assigned volunteer courier upon identity verification.

2. NGO Workflow

  1. Browse Live Directory: Filter active listings by location, food type (veg/non-veg), category, and available quantities.
  2. Atomic Quantity Reservation: Submit request for specific quantity needed. The platform atomically reserves the quantity to prevent double-booking.
  3. Approval Notification: Receive confirmation once donor approves the request.
  4. Receipt & Distribution: Receive delivery from volunteer courier, verify hygiene, and distribute to beneficiaries.

3. Volunteer Workflow

  1. Task Assignment or Claim: View approved deliveries assigned by admin or claim open pickup tasks nearby.
  2. Pickup from Donor: Navigate to donor address, collect food packets, and mark status as PICKED_UP.
  3. Transit Tracking: Update status to IN_TRANSIT while commuting to the NGO facility.
  4. Handoff Confirmation: Deliver to NGO representative, mark DELIVERED, and record confirmation notes.

Automated Lifecycle & State Machine

Entity Possible Statuses Trigger / Business Rule
Food Donation AVAILABLE, PARTIALLY_RESERVED, RESERVED, COLLECTED, DELIVERED, EXPIRED, CANCELLED Changes status based on available vs reserved quantity; automatically marked EXPIRED by background scheduler when past deadline.
Pickup Request PENDING, APPROVED, REJECTED, CANCELLED, ASSIGNED, PICKED_UP, IN_TRANSIT, COMPLETED Created by NGO; approved by Donor; completed when courier finishes physical handover.
Delivery Task ASSIGNED, PICKED_UP, IN_TRANSIT, DELIVERED, FAILED Volunteers log milestone timestamps; status transitions are checked and restricted to authorized assignees.
