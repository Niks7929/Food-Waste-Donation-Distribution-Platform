Our Mission

Rescuing Surplus Food to Feed Communities

According to global reports, billions of tons of perfectly edible food are discarded annually while millions face nutritional insecurity. The Food Waste Donation & Distribution Platform was established to bridge this tragic disconnect through real-time technology, verifiable logistics, and community coordination.

We provide commercial food donors—such as restaurants, event caterers, hotels, messes, hostels, and markets—an efficient, transparent conduit to list excess food before expiration, enabling verified grassroots NGOs to redirect nourishment to those who need it most.

Core Guiding Principles
  • Safety First: We emphasize food safety and handling protocols. Donations must specify preparation time, storage instructions, and allergens.
  • No Overbooking: Advanced database row locking prevents concurrent double-reservations of limited quantities.
  • Accountability: Every status transition, assignment, pickup, and delivery timestamp is permanently audited in historical logs.
  • Community Powered: Mobilizing local volunteer couriers to make local, zero-cost deliveries rapid and humane.

Platform Food Safety & Legal Compliance Notice

The Food Waste Donation & Distribution Platform serves strictly as a coordination infrastructure connecting voluntary donors with registered relief organizations.

Important: The platform does NOT inspect food items or certify biological safety merely because an item appears on the directory. Donors are legally obligated under local food safety regulations to donate only clean, unspoiled, properly cooled or heated food. NGOs must inspect packaging, smell, and temperature upon physical collection prior to distribution.
